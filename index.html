Chapter 01

Intro to Trading

Trading is the buying and selling of financial assets — like stocks, forex, crypto, or commodities — with the goal of making a profit. Unlike long-term investing, trading often involves shorter timeframes and more active decision-making.

📌 Key Concept
A market is any place (physical or digital) where buyers and sellers come together to exchange assets. Understanding how markets move is the foundation of all trading.

There are many types of markets you can trade in. The most popular include the stock market, the forex market, crypto, and commodities like gold and oil. Each has its own hours, risks, and opportunities.

📉
Bear Market
Prices are falling broadly — sellers outnumber buyers.
📈
Bull Market
Prices are rising broadly — buyers are in control.
🔄
Sideways Market
No clear direction — price moves within a sideways range.

Chapter 02

Reading Charts

Charts are the primary tool a trader uses to analyse price action. The most popular chart type is the candlestick chart, which shows four key data points for each time period: the Open, High, Low, and Close (OHLC).

💡 Tip
A green (bullish) candle closes higher than it opened. A red (bearish) candle closes lower. Learning to read these patterns quickly is a core skill every trader needs.

Timeframes

A candlestick represent one period of time. Timeframes can range from ticks to a month. So one candle can represent price movement of a minute or a week or a month.

We use timeframes to analyze movement and spot our areas of support and resistance, key entry levels, volatility and others.
The timeframe of your chart depends on the type of trader that you are.

Scalperstypically use the 1 to 5 minutes charts as they look for quick entries and exits.

Day traderstypically use the 15 minutes to a hour charts. They hold positions for longer than a scalper and less that a swing trader.

Swing traderstypically use the 4 hours to daily charts.

Position traderstypically use the weekly to monthly charts as they hold their entries for a longer period.
